spectral, explained

au_09 · fft
phase vocoder
turns each slice of sound into sine waves, edits them, rebuilds it
why it matters
pitch and time come apart: warp moves pitch without speed, time stretches without pitch
the trade
bigger fft = finer pitch, smeared transients, more latency · smaller = the reverse

05

usb · options.txt

au_05 · 1 = on, 0 = off
DSP_ORDER 1
1 = time domain into spectral · 0 = spectral into time domain
FREEZE_WET 0
1 = freeze jumps to full wet when mix is fully dry
LATENCY_COMP 0
1 = delays the dry to line up with the fft
ALWAYS_BLUR 1
0 = no blurring at all with time and blur fully ccw
QUANTIZE_WARP 1
semitones: 0 off · 1 knob · 2 knob and cv
WARP_DEADZONES 1
wide octave zones on the knob · 0 for smooth sweeps
any fat32 usb-a drive · the module runs without it · white LED over the port = settings loaded
